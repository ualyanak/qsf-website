The Premise

Built to study the market’s most overlooked horizon.

Fictional narrative — sample copy

The imagined firm behind this demo is founded by physicists and mathematicians shortly before the 2020 pandemic, on the premise that medium-term market behaviour resists explanation by classical models.

Short-term price action is typically driven by high-frequency trading algorithms, while long-term trends tend to follow macroeconomic forces. Neither framework effectively captures the middle ground, where retail investor behaviour can have an outsized influence. The fictional firm builds a framework grounded in quantum mechanics to navigate that middle ground.

This narrative exists to give the page realistic content to lay out. It describes no real firm, no real research programme, and no real track record.

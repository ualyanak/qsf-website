How to read these values

  • Equity and ETF prices use best-effort public delayed snapshots when available.
  • Seeded option values update automatically from delayed BULL/INFQ prices and time decay; they are model estimates, not exchange quotes.
  • Historical points are end-of-day estimates recorded nightly after completed sessions; the headline account value may use fresher delayed marks.
  • Figures are illustrative and are not official NAV, AUM, performance, or accounting records.
  • Local edits are simulations, not trades, orders, or broker instructions.
